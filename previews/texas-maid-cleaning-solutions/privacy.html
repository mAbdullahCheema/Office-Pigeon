Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

At Texas Maid Cleaning Solutions, LLC, we commit to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you visit or book a cleaning service with us.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or contact us for quotes, we collect specific personal information, including:

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and physical billing/cleaning address.
  • Service Preferences: Details about your home configuration, specialized cleaning requirements, and frequency preferences.
  • Communication Data: Inquiries, feedback, and notes regarding scheduling or requests sent directly to our support agents.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, improve, and personalize our services, including:

  • Estimating service costs and sending custom service quotes.
  • Scheduling and dispatching our cleaning teams to your property.
  • Communicating updates, confirmations, or follow-up questions.
  • Responding to inquiries or customer support messages.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or lease your personal information to third parties. We share information only with authorized service provider platforms (e.g., booking databases and communication pipelines) to deliver and coordinate your scheduled cleaning services.

4. Security of Your Data

We implement administrative and technical measures to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. However, please note that no internet transmission is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. You can make these requests by contacting us directly at our office support channels.
