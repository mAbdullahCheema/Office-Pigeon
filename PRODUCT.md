# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small and growing business owners evaluating Office Pigeon services, especially founders and operators who want a clearer online presence, faster customer replies, and practical lead capture without managing the technical details themselves.

## Product Purpose

Office Pigeon presents and sells websites, chatbots, AI calling agents, and workflow systems. Success means visitors quickly understand the offer, trust the company, choose a relevant package, and request a recommendation or consultation.

## Brand Personality

Practical, responsive, and trustworthy. The brand should feel like a capable technical partner that explains modern AI systems in plain business language.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI SaaS landing pages, vague automation buzzwords, decorative dashboards with no business meaning, unreadable low-contrast gray text, and visuals that look disconnected from customer inquiries, WhatsApp, calls, websites, or operations.

## Design Principles

- Show the system working, not only the final deliverable.
- Keep pricing and next steps clear for non-technical business owners.
- Make AI feel useful, accountable, and easy to understand.
- Use motion to explain flow and state changes, not as decoration.
- Preserve Office Pigeon's existing warm, direct, orange-led identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, readable mobile layouts, and reduced-motion accommodations for animated surfaces.
