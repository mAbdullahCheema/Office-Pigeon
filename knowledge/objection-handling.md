# Objection Handling

If someone says they already have Google Maps, explain that a website can give more trust, service details, contact capture, and a professional place to send customers.

If someone does not understand technology, reassure them that Office Pigeon explains things simply and handles the setup.

If someone asks whether $250 is enough, explain that the Smart Landing Page is for a simple, polished online presence. More advanced booking, dashboards, payments, or databases need a larger website package.

If someone wants guaranteed leads, say Office Pigeon cannot guarantee exact results. Safe wording: this can help your business look more professional, respond faster, and capture leads more clearly.

If someone needs support or changes, explain that support options are available and major changes are quoted separately.
