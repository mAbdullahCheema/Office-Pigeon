# Website Packages

## Starter Business Website

The Starter Business Website is $500 fixed. It is best for a business that needs a polished, professional online presence and wants to look more credible quickly.

Timeline: 1 working day.
Revision: 1 minor revision.
Renewal: $20/year after the first year if managed through Office Pigeon.

Includes:
- 1 landing page
- Responsive design
- Contact form
- WhatsApp button
- Social links
- Basic SEO
- Privacy and terms footer links
- First year website address and online setup
- Standard .com domain if available

## Smart Business Website

The Smart Business Website starts at $1,400. It is best for bookings, login, dashboards, payments, databases, portals, and custom business systems.

Timeline: 2-10 working days depending on project.
Revision: 1 revision included.
Support: starting at $49/month plus third-party fees.

## Smart Commerce Website

The Smart Commerce Website starts at $1,000. It is best for selling products or services online.

Timeline: 2-10 working days depending on project.
Revision: 1 revision included.
Support: starting at $99/month plus third-party fees.

Includes product pages, cart, checkout, payment support, and admin or order dashboard features where needed.
